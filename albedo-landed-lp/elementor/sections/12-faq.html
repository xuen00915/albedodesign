How long does a landed home renovation take?

It depends on your home’s size and how much work it needs. A full landed renovation takes longer than a partial one, so we agree a clear schedule with you before work starts and include it with your quotation.

How much does a landed renovation cost?

Every landed home is different, so we don’t quote a one-size price. Cost depends on floor area, scope, materials and carpentry. A free site visit gets you an itemised quotation for your home.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The consultation and site visit are free with no obligation to proceed.

Can you renovate just part of the house?

Yes. We do full-house renovations as well as kitchens, bathrooms or a single floor.

Can I send my floor plan?

Yes. Send it to us on WhatsApp after you submit the form, and your designer will review it before the consultation.
